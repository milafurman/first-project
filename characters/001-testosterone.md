## A. CHARACTER BIBLE FOR ATLABS CAST

**PRINCE CHARMING** (the body)
- Personality: the golden boy running on fumes — still polite, still trying, deeply tired, and quietly humiliated about it.
- Age look: early 40s. Handsome with mileage.
- Body type: broad frame gone comfortable — shoulders that used to fill the doublet, now slightly rounded.
- Wardrobe: royal-blue doublet with gold trim, top clasp undone; scuffed riding boots; ceremonial sword always on hip.
- Signature color: royal blue + gold.
- Face notes: square jaw with stubble, warm brown eyes with heavy lids, golden hair with ONE gray streak at the left temple.
- NEVER changes: the gray streak, the blue-and-gold doublet, the sword on the hip, the undone top clasp (it re-clasps only in the final scene — that's the arc, in wardrobe).

**TESTOSTERONE** (hero compound)
- Personality: grumpy potion-seller; looks like a bouncer who lost a fight and is still mad about it. No speeches. All results.
- Age look: ageless 50s.
- Body type: built like a cellar door — thick neck, forearms like hams, moves slow because nothing has ever made him hurry.
- Wardrobe: worn leather apron over rolled shirtsleeves; bandolier of small amber potion vials across the chest; heavy boots.
- Signature color: ember amber.
- Face notes: broken nose (set badly, twice), heavy brow, short iron-and-pepper beard, one scar through the right eyebrow, resting scowl.
- NEVER changes: the broken nose, the amber bandolier, the scowl baseline (his "smile" in the finale is a 5% eyebrow lift, nothing more).

**THE FOG OF DECLINE** (villain — a character/weather, never a body part)
- Personality: passive-aggressive weather. Never roars, never fights fair — it settles, dims, and acts like it pays rent.
- Look: slate-gray mist with slow curling tendrils; enters LOW, along floors and over shoulders; two faint dim glows deep in the bank where eyes would be — never a mouth, never a face.
- Signature color: desaturating gray — color visibly drains from whatever it touches (this is the series' visual grammar for the villain).
- NEVER changes: always enters low; never has a mouth; never touches Testosterone (it recoils from amber light).

**THE MIRROR** (the enabler)
- Personality: sycophantic court flatterer; lies to keep the peace, deeply relieved when finally allowed to stop.
- Look: full-length gilded baroque mirror; its "face" is a soft smear of light in the glass.
- Signature colors: candle-gold glow when lying; clean silver daylight when telling the truth.
- NEVER changes: the gilded frame. Only the light in the glass changes — gold (lie) to silver (truth) is the Mirror's entire character arc.

**GERTA THE BAKER** (background extra 1)
- Round, flour-dusted, 50s, headscarf, always holding a pie. Sees everything, says nothing, communicates entirely in eyebrow. Signature color: warm bread-crust brown. Never changes: the pie.

**PIP THE STABLE BOY** (background extra 2)
- 14, freckles, straw-colored hair sticking out of a cap, devoted to the Prince's horse and personally offended that it won't wake up. Signature color: hay green. Never changes: the cap.

## B. VISUAL STYLE LOCK FOR ATLABS

- **Style (one, locked):** storybook 3D cartoon, slightly gritty — painterly textures, worn fabric, chipped stone, warm imperfections. Shrek-adjacent character exaggeration. NOT cutesy-baby, NOT photoreal, NOT neon.
- **Lighting:** warm hearth-and-candle interiors as the kingdom's baseline; wherever the Fog sits, light goes flat, cold, and gray. Testosterone carries his own light — everything near him warms toward amber. The finale is full golden-hour.
- **Palette:** royal blue + gold (kingdom), slate gray (Fog), ember amber (Testosterone). The episode's color story: gold → gray → amber → gold.
- **Creative body integration — ALLOWED:** fog rolling over the Prince's shoulders; fog swallowing the throne; color desaturating around him; the sword visibly "gaining weight" (drooping arm, dragging tip); the horse asleep standing up.
- **FORBIDDEN:** organs with faces, syringes, injection visuals, molecular structures, glowing-bloodstream shots, anatomy cutaways, before/after body morphs. The Prince's body never changes shape on screen — the Fog leaves; that's the transformation.

## H. SCIENCE CARD (educational only — no dosing, no how-to-obtain)

1. **Testosterone is a steroid hormone the body builds from cholesterol.** — Hall & Hall, *Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology*, 14th ed., ch. 81.
2. **The brain runs the show:** the pituitary gland sends a messenger hormone (LH) that tells the body to make testosterone — production is commanded, not spontaneous. — Guyton & Hall, 14th ed., ch. 81–82; Khan Academy, "The hypothalamus and pituitary gland."
3. **The Fog is real and it's slow:** in men, testosterone declines gradually — on the order of ~1% per year from roughly the 30s onward — which is why nobody notices the year it happens, only the decade. — Harman SM, et al., Baltimore Longitudinal Study of Aging, *J Clin Endocrinol Metab.* 2001;86(2):724–731.
4. **It's a builder, not just a mood:** testosterone increases muscle size and strength and, with the estrogen made from it, helps maintain bone density. — Bhasin S, et al., *N Engl J Med.* 1996;335:1–7; Finkelstein JS, et al., *N Engl J Med.* 2013;369:1011–1022.
5. **Low testosterone has a face:** fatigue, low mood, reduced libido, and loss of muscle are recognized symptoms of clinically low testosterone — which is a diagnosable, treatable medical condition, not a character flaw. — Bhasin S, et al., "Testosterone Therapy in Men with Hypogonadism: An Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guideline," *J Clin Endocrinol Metab.* 2018;103(5):1715–1744.
