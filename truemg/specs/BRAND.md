# TrueMG — the locked visual system

Source: **The Peptide Files**, section 01, compiled 16 Sept 2026. Mila's own
artifact. This supersedes every visual recommendation made before it.

> **Black. White. Strong. Truth. Touches of gold.**
>
> Stark pure black and white; heavy grotesk display; mono for all data. Gold is
> the single luxury signal — a hairline rule, an outlined word, a price cut, one
> vial detail. *Never a wash, never cream, never soft.*

The display sample in that system reads **"TRUE to the milligram."** True · MG.
That is the brand's own line, and it is the strongest piece of copy anyone has
written for this company.

## Palette — six tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#000000` | Ground, authority |
| Paper | `#FCFBF8` | Inverted band |
| Gold | `#D8B46A` | Accent on ink |
| Gold deep | `#9C7C34` | Accent on paper |
| Gold soft | `#ECD6A0` | Editorial italics |
| Fg on ink | `#EDEBE6` | Body text on dark |
| Green | `#5AA77B` | **Price cuts only.** Nothing else. |

**The rule, verbatim:** gold appears *once per view*, as a signal, never as
decoration. No cream. No pastels. No cutesy serif. No emoji-as-icons. No mascots.

## Type — three roles

| Role | Face | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Helvetica Neue / Arial Black, weight 900, tracking −0.03 to −0.04em | Headlines. Tight, monolithic, unhurried |
| Editorial | Newsreader italic 400 (Google Fonts) | Used sparingly, inside display |
| Data | SF Mono / Menlo / Consolas | Every dose, hex, gram and **price**. "It is the receipt." |

All three are either system faces or on Google Fonts, so all three are reachable
from `customCss` with no blocked font host.

## Voice

> We name the lab. We print the price. Every claim carries its grade and its
> source. That's the whole pitch.

Strong, plain, unhurried. No hype, no glow, no hedging.

## The signature device — the receipt

A spec-sheet card: *the whole label, printed.* Rows of label/value in mono, then
the price in the display face at full size, with the saving as an outlined gold
pill. This is the single best conversion asset the brand already owns, and it is
a better answer than copying the reference competitor's discount badge.

**It needs one edit before it can ship on the store.** The reference version of
the receipt carries `Dose`, `Evidence` and `Cycle` rows. Those are protocol
guidance and cannot appear on a research-use storefront at any price. Ship the
receipt with `Compound`, `Source`, `Purity`, `Lot`, `Format` and `Price`. Drop
the other three. The device survives the edit intact — arguably it gets stronger,
because every remaining row is a verifiable fact about the material rather than a
claim about a body.

## The mark — resolved

**The blue is dead.** The only element carried forward from the old logo is the
lizard, and it was never a seahorse: it is a **Gila monster**, and it is the most
defensible idea this company owns.

In 1992 a Bronx VA endocrinologist, **John Eng**, isolated a peptide from Gila
monster venom and named it **exendin-4**. Human GLP-1 breaks down in the blood in
about two minutes, which made it useless as a medicine. The lizard's version
lasted hours — because a Gila monster eats only a few times a year and evolved a
hormone patient enough to wring every calorie from a rare meal. The VA declined
to patent the finding. Eng patented it himself, licensed it, and it reached the
market as Byetta in 2005. Every GLP-1 since descends from it.

The mark is therefore not decoration and not a mascot. It is a citation.

Three directions are drawn and published at `../assets/gila-mark.html`:

| | Direction | Where it belongs |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Beaded silhouette** — flat gold, tubercular scales knocked out | The brand mark. The only one that holds at favicon size |
| B | **Banded specimen** — the real blotched cross-banding | About page, printed large. Dies below ~28px |
| C | **The seal** — silhouette in a hairline ring reading `EXENDIN-4 · 1992` | The vial label and beside the certificate block |

All three are vector and recolour to any token in the system. The drawing is a
first pass: the beading is the right idea and the proportions are close, but a
mark this load-bearing is worth an illustrator's hand before it is final.

### The one hard limit on the story
On the storefront, the Gila monster is **provenance for the name and the mark,
only**. The history of exendin-4 is a fact about a scientific discovery. It must
never be placed where it reads as a claim about what any compound sold here does
in a person — not in copy, not in a caption, not in alt text, not in a meta
description. The lizard explains where the science came from. It does not
describe the product.

### Still open: the logo on the ink ground
The published logo is dark artwork, so on black "TRUE" vanishes and the old blue
"MG" floats alone. The palette stays held until the new mark lands.

## Where it does NOT apply

The Peptide Files is a private reference document containing dosing tables,
reconstitution maths and cycling protocols. **None of that content goes near the
storefront.** Take the visual system. Leave the contents.
