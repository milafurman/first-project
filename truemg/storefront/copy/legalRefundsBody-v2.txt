All orders are packaged and documented for laboratory research use, with certificates of analysis available for verification prior to or upon shipment. Shipping timelines and carrier options are confirmed at checkout.

Because products are laboratory reagents, TrueMG inspects and verifies orders prior to dispatch. Any report of a damaged shipment or a documented fulfillment error must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery, with your order number and photographs of the parcel and the vial as they arrived. Approved claims are resolved via replacement or credit at TrueMG's discretion.

Returns of intact orders are not accepted. Once material has left our control it cannot be re-verified against its certificate or returned to stock, so an unopened vial cannot be resold as documented research material. This is why the certificate for every lot is published before you order rather than after.

Orders confirmed as inconsistent with research-use terms will not be fulfilled. Contact our support channel for any shipping or order-accuracy concern.
