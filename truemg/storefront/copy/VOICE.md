# TrueMG's voice — the lizard is the brief

Everyone in this category shouts. Neon, urgency timers, stacked superlatives,
"unlock your potential". The opening is to be **the calm one**, and the brand
already hands us the character for it.

## The animal is the voice

A Gila monster is heavy, slow, and famously unhurried. It does not chase. It is
the least impressive-looking animal in the desert and it is where the
interesting molecule turned out to be. Four traits fall straight out of that:

| Trait | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Unhurried** | No countdown timers, no "only 3 left", no urgency. Ever. |
| **Observant** | Numbers, not adjectives. "99.4%, HPLC" beats "ultra-pure". |
| **Dry** | Understated and a little deadpan. **No exclamation marks.** |
| **Unimpressed** | Never oversells. The restraint IS the claim. |

**The refusal to rush is a position, not just a tone.** A competitor's page runs
a countdown that resets forever; that is an FTC problem as well as a tell. Not
running one says something true about us, for free.

## The test for any sentence

> Would a careful researcher who has read a hundred vendor pages believe this?

If it needs an exclamation mark to land, it isn't true enough yet.

# Applied — the microcopy, field by field

These are real Lapis copy keys, not inventions.

## `announcementBar`
Currently: *"Your COA is filed to the lot number stamped on your vial. Check one
against the other."*

Keep it. It is already the voice: a specific, checkable, unexcited claim. It
invites verification instead of asking for trust.

## `searchTitle` / `searchPlaceholder` / `searchCta`
```
Search the catalog
Compound, or a lot number
Search
```
*"Or a lot number" is the whole brand in four words — it assumes you have a vial
in your hand and want to check it.*

## `catalogEmptyNote`
```
Nothing matches that. Compound names, abbreviations and lot numbers all work.
```

## `pdTestedLabel`
```
Independently analysed
```
*Not "Lab Verified" — "verified" is a word anyone can print. "Independently
analysed" states who did it and admits it is an analysis, not a blessing.*

## `pdDocsLabel`

On file
```

## `productBulkNote`
```
Ten vials or more of one size, ask us — sales@truemglabs.com

*No "DISCOUNT!!" No percentage dangled. Just the door.*

## `aboutCtaHeading` / `aboutCtaSub` / `aboutCtaButton`

Pick anything in the catalog and check its paperwork before you check its price.
Open the catalog

*Telling someone to look at the documents before the prices is a flex no

*Dry, and a real differentiator in six words.*

# Banned, permanently

- **"performance"** — standing brand rule, never ships.
- Exclamation marks in site copy.
- Urgency: countdowns, "only N left", "ends tonight".
- Anything about what a compound does in a person or an animal.
- "Unlock", "optimise", "biohack", "next-level", "game-changing".
- Superlatives without a number behind them.

# The one thing to protect

The current About line **"Anyone can fill a vial"** is the best sentence on the
site. Everything else should be measured against it: concrete, slightly blunt,
and impossible to say unless it is true.
