## What changed, and why

**1. "within a reasonable window of delivery" → "within 48 hours of delivery".**
The FAQ already promised 48 hours. The policy gave no number at all, so a
day-four claim was simultaneously inside the policy and outside the FAQ. They now
agree, and the policy also picked up the FAQ's photograph and order-number
requirement so a customer reading either one gets the same instruction.

**2. A returns position, which did not exist.** The footer link is labelled
**"Shipping & Returns"** and the page said nothing about returns — it covered
transit damage and fulfilment errors only. Support would have been asked and had
nothing to give.

The new paragraph says no, and says why in a way that works *for* the brand: an
unopened vial cannot be re-verified against its certificate once it has left our
control, so it cannot be resold as documented research material. **That turns a
refusal into evidence of the documentation standard** — and it lands the
certificate-before-you-order point again.
