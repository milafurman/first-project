THE LITERATURE THIS CATALOG RESTS ON

TrueMG Labs makes exactly two claims about any compound: that it is the molecule named on the label, and that an independent laboratory measured it. Both claims rest on published science, so the science is listed here rather than alluded to.

Two kinds of reference appear below and nothing else. Identity papers are the work that first isolated, sequenced or structurally described a compound. Method papers are the published analytical chemistry used to confirm identity and measure purity. No paper here is cited for what a compound does in a living thing, because that is not something this company has any business telling you.

WHERE THIS LIST IS THIN, AND WHY

Five compounds in the catalog carry no reference above: BPC-157, KPV, SS-31, MOTS-C and SELANK. In every case the reason is the same. Either the compound was never the subject of a separate isolation or synthesis paper — KPV is simply three residues of a larger peptide, not a separately discovered molecule — or the available literature describes what the compound did in an experiment rather than what the compound is. This page does not carry that second kind of paper, so those entries stay empty.

Leaving them empty is the point. A reference list assembled to look complete would be the easiest thing on this site to fake, and the hardest thing for a reader to check. Identity and purity for those five lots are established the same way as for every other lot: by the methods listed above, on the certificate filed against your lot number.
