1. What does “for laboratory research use only” mean?
Every compound TrueMG Labs lists is supplied strictly as reference material for in-vitro laboratory research. It is not for human or veterinary use, and nothing on this site should be read as guidance for any use in a person or an animal. Placing an order is your confirmation that the material will be handled in a laboratory setting by someone qualified to do so.

2. Do you provide a certificate of analysis?
Yes. Each lot is analysed by an independent laboratory and the certificate is filed against that lot number. The certificate you receive matches the vial in your hand, not a representative sample from a different production run. Published certificates are on the Lab Results page, and any certificate not yet posted can be requested by email with the lot number.

3. What is actually tested?
Identity and purity. Identity confirms the material is the compound named on the label; purity is reported as a percentage with the method stated on the certificate. Both figures come from a laboratory with no commercial interest in the result.

4. How quickly do orders ship?
Orders placed on a business day leave within 48 hours. You receive tracking as soon as the parcel is handed over. Orders placed over a weekend or a public holiday go out on the next business day.

5. How is everything packaged?
Vials are sealed, crimped and packed in rigid protective material inside a plain outer box with no branding describing the contents. Each vial carries its own label with the compound, the quantity, the lot number and the research-use statement.

6. Who is allowed to order?
Orders are accepted from qualified researchers and laboratory or institutional accounts who are at least 18 years of age and who accept the Terms of Service. You are responsible for compliance with the laws and regulations that apply where you are.

7. Can I ask for a compound you do not list?
Yes. There is a request form at the bottom of the catalog. Tell us the compound and the quantity you are looking for; if it is something we can source and document to the same standard, we will come back to you with availability.

8. What if a vial arrives damaged?
Report it within 48 hours of delivery. Email us your order number, photographs of the parcel and the vial as they arrived, and one continuous, unedited video of the package being opened — starting with the sealed parcel, showing the shipping label, then the contents and any damage. Claims without that video cannot be approved. Approved claims are resolved by replacement. The full terms are on the Shipping & Refunds page.

9. How do I pay?
The payment options available to you are shown at checkout. If a method you need is not listed, contact us before ordering and we will tell you whether it can be arranged.

10. Why is a Gila monster on the label?
It is a nod to where a great deal of peptide science began. In the 1990s researchers studying the venom of the Gila monster, a slow desert lizard native to the American southwest, isolated and characterised a peptide that went on to become one of the most studied molecules in the field. The lizard is on our mark as a reminder that the interesting material tends to come from careful observation of something nobody else was looking at. It is the story behind the name, nothing more.
