## Visual system — the actual lesson
- **No white boxes.** Every product image sits on a soft color field keyed to that
  vial's own label color — lilac for GLP-3, blue for GLP-2, red for GLP-1, mint for
  GHK-Cu. The background *is* the brand.
- **Vials are shot at an angle, floating, with a real cast shadow.** Never flat,
  never centered, never in a frame.
- **Asymmetric split sections** — image bleeds to one edge, copy holds the other.
  No boxed content column.
- **Gradient fields per section** so the page changes temperature as you scroll.
  One section is a full field of dozens of vials scattered at depth and blurred.
- Palette is soft and pastel over white, with near-black type and a black primary
  button. It reads premium and reads female-friendly without reading like a spa —
  the discipline is that the *type* stays hard while the *color* stays soft.

## Do NOT copy
- Their product names and label art — that is their trade dress.
- Any mechanic we cannot honor. A countdown that resets forever when there is no real
  rotation is the kind of thing that draws an FTC complaint, and it is the one piece
  of their site that only works *because* theirs is real.
- Their pastel-only palette wholesale. TrueMG needs to land for men too; the move is
  their *structure* (color field per product, no white boxes) with our own temperature.
