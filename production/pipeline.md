# PRODUCTION PIPELINE — SUNO + ATLABS

This is the series' locked pipeline. It supersedes the earlier Hedra pipeline doc (removed;
in git history if ever needed).

1. **Claude** — script, rhyme, character bible, scene list, Suno prompt, Atlabs brief.
   Claude does not invent new episode plots unless asked; approved stories only.
2. **Suno** — the song (each episode is a music video; the song IS the spine).
3. **Atlabs** — the finished animated episode. Paste the Suno URL / MP3 into the Music Video
   workflow, lock the Cast from the episode's character bible, generate short cut first, then
   the YouTube cut from the same Cast.
4. **Optional Higgsfield or Runway** — one or two cinematic insert shots only, never the whole
   episode. Generated last, matched to the locked Atlabs cast.

## HARD RULES (production-side restatement of the series bible)

- Body is never the villain. Villain is always the process (aging, decline, fatigue,
  perimenopause, metabolic chaos, entropy).
- Characters look like fairy-tale people — never organs, syringes, cells, or medical diagrams.
- One peptide / steroid / supplement per episode as the main storybook character.
- Gender the body-protagonist by who benefits most from that compound.
- Tone: Shrek humor, elevated. PG-13 ceiling. Thick innuendo for adults, clean surface for
  Meta / TikTok review. No explicit sex jokes, no nudity, no drug-use how-to.
- Short cut: under 50 seconds (TikTok / Reels / Shorts, 9:16).
- Long cut: YouTube (16:9), same story with more scenes and a short monologue from the
  compound character.
- Facts accurate and cited (textbook / Khan Academy / peer-reviewed). No Wikipedia. No
  "studies show."
- Never sneak anatomy diagrams into animation notes.

## PER-EPISODE DELIVERABLE (what Claude produces for every approved story)

A. Character bible for Atlabs Cast (name, personality, age look, body type, wardrobe,
   signature color, face notes, what never changes)
B. Visual style lock for Atlabs
C. Suno production brief (title, style tags, full 50s lyrics, YouTube extra verse,
   negative style notes)
D. Atlabs music-video brief (lengths, timed scene list, cast mapping, caption/karaoke
   notes, 9:16 + 16:9 reframe notes)
E. Short-cut script with timing beats (0–3 hook, story, punchline, logo)
F. YouTube add-on (extra scenes, villain origin line, compound monologue)
G. Optional insert shots (max 2; prompt, duration, cast-match requirements)
H. Science card (5 plain-English facts + sources; no dosing, no sourcing)
I. Human next-steps checklist

Episode files live in `characters/NNN-<compound>.md` and contain sections A–I.
